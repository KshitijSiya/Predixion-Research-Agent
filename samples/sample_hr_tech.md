# Research Report: Find 5 Indian B2B SaaS startups in HR tech and summarize their positioning.

**Confidence Level:** `High` (85% verified)

## Executive Summary
The Indian B2B SaaS HR tech landscape includes Belong.co, Betterplace, DarwinBox, greytHR, and Instahyre, each positioning itself around data‑driven talent acquisition, unified HR operations, end‑to‑end HCM suites, payroll automation, and AI‑powered recruitment respectively.

## Key Findings
1. Belong.co positions its ML‑enabled platform as a data‑driven outbound hiring solution that scans 90+ professional networks to match talent for enterprise clients.
2. Betterplace markets a unified, data‑driven HR suite covering recruitment, onboarding, verification, attendance and payroll, targeting mid‑market and enterprise customers across APAC, India and GCC.
3. DarwinBox positions itself as a cloud‑native, full‑stack HCM platform offering recruitment, payroll, performance and employee engagement tools for large enterprises, highlighted by its unicorn status.
4. greytHR focuses on automating payroll and core HR processes for SMBs and larger firms, emphasizing ease of use, compliance and a large customer base across 25+ countries.
5. Instahyre offers an AI‑driven recruitment platform with its InstaMatch technology, promising to cut hiring time by 60‑70% and costs by up to 90% for tech hiring at scale.
